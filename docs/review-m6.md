## A. Confirmed defects (reproduced; scripts in `/tmp/m6-review-scratch/`, run with `node <file>`: Node 24 strips types, fixtures use temp dirs, no network)

### B1 [should-fix] A new exercise first logged with no weight is `bodyweight` for good; later loaded sets are judged by reps and never show progress
- Where: `packages/extensions/src/health/store.ts:373,404-405` (`inferKind` from the first call's sets), `:427-449` (`resolveExercise` stores it), `tools.ts:236-243` (the "New exercise created" line names the exercise, not the kind), `stats.ts:108-110` (`metricOf`: bodyweight = reps).
- Evidence `s04-bodyweight-kind.ts` (b): "cable crossover" 3x12 with no weight on 20 Oct, then 3x12 at 15 kg (23 Oct) and 20 kg (25 Oct). The row stays `kind = bodyweight`; the sets read "3x12 +15 kg", "+20 kg"; `exercise_progress` says "Best day (most reps in a set): 12 reps … Change … 0 reps … No new personal record".
- Mechanism: kind is inferred once and nothing changes it; there is no rename/merge/kind tool. The only way out is to delete every workout of the exercise (an exercise with no set is removed), one approval each. One omitted `weight` (a machine, "20s" said for dumbbells) poisons the exercise silently, and neither the model nor the owner is told which kind was chosen.
- Fix (any one): say the kind in the new-exercise line ("treated as bodyweight because no weight was given; if it has a load, delete this workout and log it again with the weights"); or refuse a new, non-catalog name with no weight unless the call says `bodyweight: true`; or let a later set with `kg > 0` on a non-catalog bodyweight exercise turn it into `weighted`. Add a test.

### B2 [should-fix] Added weight on a bodyweight exercise is invisible to progress, and the Health rules tell the model not to give any
- Where: `stats.ts:108-115` (`metricOf`, `compareSets`: the load only breaks ties), `catalog.ts` (Pull-up, Chin-up, Push-up, Dip are `bodyweight`), `apps/server/src/agents.ts:104` ("Bodyweight exercises (pull-ups, dips) take no weight.") against `tools.ts:177` (the `weight` parameter: "for one with a belt or vest, the added weight").
- Evidence `s04` (a): dips 8 reps at body weight, 8 x +20 kg, 5 x +30 kg gives "Change … -3 reps", "No new personal record", "Best day … 8 reps on Mon 19 Oct" (the unloaded day), "Total reps: 63". By any measure that counts load the owner got stronger. Loaded pull-ups and dips are how most people progress past ten reps, and "how much am I picking up" is the stated purpose.
- Fix (cheapest): rule line and tool description say "loaded pull-ups/dips: log them as 'Weighted pull-up' / 'Weighted dip' with the load" (the inferred kind is then `weighted`; `s25`: 10, 15, 20 kg tracks with records); better: judge a bodyweight exercise by (added kg, reps) when any set carries a load. Add a test for the chosen behaviour.

### B3 [should-fix] A catalog "weighted" name cannot be logged without a load, and the error offers no way out
- Where: `tools.ts:220-224` (`checkLoads`), `catalog.ts` (Squat, Lunge, Calf raise, Hip thrust, Leg curl … are `weighted`).
- Evidence `s16-bodyweight-variants.ts`: `lunges`, `calf raises`, `squats`, `hip thrusts` at 3x12, with and without `weight: 0`, all answer "lunges is a weighted exercise: every set needs a weight. Ask the owner what it was." Nothing is saved. The owner says "no weight, bodyweight lunges" and the model has nothing to try except inventing another name.
- Fix: extend the message ("If it was done without a load, log it as a different exercise, for example 'Bodyweight lunge'"), or take an explicit `weight: 0` for a catalog-weighted name as "create `Bodyweight <name>` (kind bodyweight)".

## C. Nits

- **N1 Displayed averages are occasionally 0.1 kg off.** `stats.ts:45` rounds the 7-day average to 2 decimals and `reports.ts:99,123,161`, `facts.ts:44`, `index.ts:35` round that again to 1; `stats.ts:78,83,88` take the trend and change from already-rounded or float values. `s01`: 72.44 and 72.45 give "7-day average: 72.5 kg" (exact 72.445, correct 72.4); 6,446 of 200,000 random windows (3.2 %) show a 1-decimal average 0.1 above the exact one. `s18` against an exact rational oracle (two seeds, 294 histories): 2-7 % show a different 7-day average, 5-7 % a different trend, and in one seed 3 % a different change (float noise on half-way values: 71.35 − 71.2 gives 0.1, exact 0.15). Total volume of lb histories is the sum of rounded daily volumes, up to 0.2 kg off (`s17`). Fix: keep `avg7` unrounded (or in integer hundredths) and round once where shown.
- **N2 Running-sum drift:** `stats.ts:34-46` adds and subtracts floats for the whole history, so after thousands of weighings a half-way window rounds down by 0.01 (`s02b/c`: 9 of 8,000 windows; 0 of the first 365). Sum the window afresh (at most 7 numbers).
- **N3 The 90-point cap is not kept for exercise charts:** `reports.ts:432-433` keeps `thin(days, 90)` **plus** every record day. `s05`: 300 days with a record each give 300 points (31,115 bytes, no `thinned`); a record every third day gives 160 points (`thinned: true`). Thin the union to 90 (keep first, last and the newest records) or change the claim. Mutant M23 (records not kept) survives: no test thins an exercise chart.
- **N4 Two sessions on one day announce two records:** `store.ts:375` judges against every set up to and including the workout's own day, `reports.ts:278` uses it. `s03`: 105 x 5 then 110 x 5 on the 25th says "new PR … (was 116.7)" and "new PR … (was 122.5)"; `exercise_progress` and the wrap-up say one record that day, "was 116.7". Judge against days before the workout's day (`date < input.date`) and flag a record only if no earlier session of that day already did, or document the per-session rule.
- **N5 A replayed `log_workout` loses its flags:** `tools.ts:234` and `store.ts:367` rebuild from the stored session with `written: []`, so after a crash replay the model text and the card lack the "new PR" and the "New exercise created … tell the owner" lines (`s21`). That line is the net for a misnamed exercise.
- **N6 `propose_delete_weight` reads the clock before it looks for its own request** (`tools.ts:380-381`). Replayed after midnight with `today`/`yesterday` it errors, or resolves another day (and then `approvals.propose` returns the original request by call id, so no wrong deletion). Rare; the doc claim is the problem.
- **N7 Absurd ranges mis-resolve silently:** `range.ts:16` allows four digits; `Date.UTC` maps years 0-99 to 1900-1999 and negative years break `addDays`. `s23`: `5000y` answers "No weight is logged between Wed 11 Oct 2147 and Sun 25 Oct 2026." with a weight logged; `2000y` is really 100 years. Cap the count (for example 120 months, 100 years) or refuse a `from` after `to` in the `range` branch too.
- **N8 Alias gaps:** `catalog.ts:73-78` strips `s`/`es` but not `ies`, so "cable flies" and "dumbbell flies" create new exercises while "cable fly" is Chest fly (`s13`); `exerciseKey` keeps only a-z0-9, so a non-Latin name gets "has no letters or digits" (`store.ts:438`, false) and "Тяга blocks" collapses to "blocks". No two catalog entries clash after plural stripping (`s13`, 0 clashes).
- **N9** `vitest.config.ts:5` names `apps/web/test/health-charts.test.ts`; the file is `health-cards.test.ts`.
- **N10 `heightCm` rides in `config.profile`**, which `runtime.ts:55` passes whole to `createProfileExtension`. Safe today only because `renderProfile` reads three fields; a future spread would tell every agent the height. Keep it out of `ProfileConfig` (the Health deps already get it separately).
- **N11 A replaced weighing keeps no history** (`replaceWeight`, ledger has call id and day only), and **there is no aggregate** such as "total volume this month": the rules forbid the model to add `list_workouts` numbers. Gaps, not defects.
- **N12** The card's "latest days" list (`health-charts.tsx:172`, `card.points.slice(-5)`) is taken from the thinned points, so for a thinned chart it is not the five latest days.
